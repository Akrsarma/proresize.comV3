Privacy Policy

Your images stay under your control.

This policy explains what ProResize does with your images, what information may be processed, and how privacy is handled.

Effective Date

Privacy policy for the ProResize website.

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy describes how the ProResize website handles information when you use its image tools. The tools run in your browser and do not require an account.

1. Image processing

When you select an image, your browser processes it locally for compression, resizing, cropping, presets, and social media exports. The website code does not upload your selected image to ProResize servers or store the image on an account.

2. Input and output files

The website accepts common image formats, including WebP, as input. Its compression and resize tools can export JPEG, PNG, or PDF. PDF files are generated in the browser from the processed image. Generated files remain on your device until you choose to download them.

You are responsible for the images you choose to process and for how you use exported files.

3. Chrome extension

The ProResize Chrome extension is a separate product and may have different permissions and data handling from this website. Review the details on its Chrome Web Store listing before installing or using it.

4. Cookies, local storage, and preferences

The website uses browser local storage to remember your selected theme preference. It does not use this preference to track you across websites.

5. Third-party services

The website uses hosting infrastructure, loads fonts from Google Fonts, and links to the Chrome Web Store. These services may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy practices.

6. Data security

Image processing happens in your browser, but visiting the website and its third-party resources involves internet requests. Keep your browser up to date and use a secure device when handling sensitive images.

7. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect feature changes, legal requirements, project deadlines, or service improvements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how ProResize handles privacy, you can contact: support@proresize.com
